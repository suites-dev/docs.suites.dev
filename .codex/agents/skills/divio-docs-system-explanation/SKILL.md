# Divio Explanation Pages

Use this skill for overview and conceptual pages whose primary job is helping a
reader understand a product or system.

Read the [Divio explanation guide](./divio-docs-system-explanation.md)
completely before substantial drafting or review. Treat it as writing-method
evidence rather than product authority.

On an explanation page:

- Establish why the topic matters and how its parts relate.
- Use one concrete example or compact visual to anchor abstract framing.
- Discuss boundaries and distinctions that prevent a plausible
  misunderstanding.
- Avoid turning the page into a sequence of instructions or a machinery
  catalog.
- Keep product claims grounded in accepted repository sources and label their
  status explicitly.
