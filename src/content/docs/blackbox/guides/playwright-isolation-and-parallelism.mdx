Two tests can run next to each other in Playwright while representing very different behavioral relationships. They may be independent, share declared state, form a comparison, or express concurrent work inside one case.

Blackbox uses the declared **test topology** to preserve those relationships without mixing evidence. Playwright scheduling alone cannot provide that meaning.

## Topology Expresses a Behavioral Relationship

Independent cases have no declared relationship, so each attempt needs its own runtime and evidence boundary. An ordered group declares that later members depend on state created earlier, while still keeping each member separately verdictable.

An explicit comparison connects named cases around one controlled difference. Concurrent operations inside one case belong to that case's synchronization contract rather than to the runner's worker placement.

## Scheduling Is Not Topology

Serial execution does not establish that two cases share state. Parallel execution does not make them independent. Workers, shards, files, titles, traces, and timing describe physical execution, not relationships in the behavioral contract.

This distinction lets Blackbox change physical placement without changing what the cases mean or which evidence belongs to them.

## Retries Must Not Rewrite History

A retry is a new physical attempt. It receives fresh runtime and evidence identity, while the earlier outcome remains part of the history.

For an ordered group, retrying only the failed member could reuse uncertain state from the earlier execution. Restarting the declared group preserves the relationship that made the sequence meaningful.

## Comparisons Need Declared Members

ODC and other comparisons depend on knowing exactly which cases belong together and what dimension differs. Inferring members from nearby execution or similar output could create a relationship that no person approved.

Blackbox therefore preserves comparisons as declared relationships rather than aggregating evidence after the run.

The [Capture Lifecycle and Evidence reference](/docs/blackbox/reference/capture-lifecycle-and-correlation#execution-topologies) lists the exact topology and retry rules.
