If a product-cache case fails and retries, the retry receives a new sandbox lease, attempt, window, capture sessions, evidence, and teardown. Blackbox never merges the failed attempt into the retry.

## Bound Every Physical Attempt

An independent case attempt owns:

- One fresh sandbox lease.
- Fresh runtime and capture identities.
- One evidence boundary.
- Its own teardown or quarantined lease.

Parallel and serial scheduling cannot make independent attempts share assurance state.

## Separate Telemetry From Assurance Control

The **telemetry plane** carries observed OTLP operations. The **assurance control plane** carries identity, participation, capability, completion, loss, cutoff, settlement, and seal facts.

Blackbox needs both. A received span cannot prove lifecycle completeness, and a lifecycle receipt cannot establish that an effect occurred.

## Capture in a Fixed Order

Before capture starts, Blackbox allocates the sandbox, admits the exact participant census, and confirms capture-protocol readiness for every required participant.

The capture lifecycle then preserves this order:

```text
Capture armed
→ Window prepared and opened
→ Required participants enter
→ Runner starts and reaches a terminal result
→ Claim-specific completion is established
→ Capture cutoff and crossing work settle
→ Capture drains
→ Participants and receiver seal
```

Readiness, runner completion, application completion, cutoff, settlement, drain, and sealing remain distinct facts.

## Assemble Evidence Without Rewriting Producers

The per-attempt evidence keeps exact references to:

- Accepted plan and selected realization.
- Sandbox lease, participants, services, processes, and isolates.
- Runner and visible assertion facts.
- Lifecycle, completion, loss, cutoff, settlement, and seal facts.
- Redacted raw-evidence references and normalized effect facts.
- Executed artifacts and verified mapping results.

The evidence assembler verifies and joins producer facts. It does not impersonate a producer or author a claim verdict.

## Derive Completeness Per Claim

Completeness depends on the exact claim direction and scope. Blackbox derives it from authenticated participant, capability, lifecycle, loss, settlement, and seal facts.

An empty effect list, a successful OTLP request, queue polling, or flush alone cannot establish completeness. [See how incomplete evidence limits claim evaluation](/docs/blackbox/reference/effects-and-claims#evidence-limits-the-conclusion).

## Execution Topologies

| Declared relationship                   | Topology                                | Evidence boundary                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cases do not share state                | Independent attempts                    | Fresh sandbox lease, runtime identity, capture, evidence, and teardown per attempt |
| Cases share declared state in order     | Ordered group                           | One fresh group lease with separate member attempts and evidence                   |
| Named cases form an approved comparison | Explicit comparison                     | Exact members and controlled dimension without evidence aggregation                |
| Operations overlap inside one case      | Typed parallel branches inside one case | One case owns the synchronization contract and evidence window                     |

Serial or parallel runner placement does not change the declared topology. Workers, shards, files, titles, traces, and timing do not create evidence relationships.

## Ordered State, Retries, and Comparisons

Cases share state only inside an explicit ordered group. The group declares member order, shared domains, transfer attestations, stop-on-failure behavior, and final cleanup.

A group retry restarts the full group on a fresh lease. Earlier attempts and outcomes remain visible.

Comparisons name exact members and an outcome-independent eligible-attempt policy. Blackbox does not aggregate evidence by flow name, worker, trace, file, title, or timing overlap.
