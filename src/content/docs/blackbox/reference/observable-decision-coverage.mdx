:::note Alpha Capability
Observable Decision Coverage is optional per run. It remains separate from ordinary effect findings and cannot change them.
:::

Use this page to look up the exact inputs and result vocabulary for Observable Decision Coverage (ODC).

## Comparison Inputs

An approved ODC comparison names:

- The exact member cases.
- The dimension that varies.
- The dimensions held constant.
- The decision scope.
- The effects included in the projection.

ODC cannot infer comparison members from test names, traces, workers, timing, execution order, or observed similarity.

## Availability

ODC terminal availability is exactly one of:

- `not-requested`
- `available`
- `unavailable`
- `unsupported`
- `integrity-failure`

A run may omit ODC. `not-requested` does not mean that ordinary runtime evidence is missing.

## Classification

When availability is `available`, diagnostic classification is exactly one of:

- `propagating`
- `partially-propagating`
- `masking-candidate`
- `coverage-gap`
- `inconsistent`
- `undecidable`

Availability and diagnostic classification remain separate fields and namespaces. `requested` is a selection and admission fact, not a terminal result.

An unknown diagnostic classification fails closed as ODC `integrity-failure`. `snapshot-failed` maps to `unavailable` with a reason, while `unsupported-topology` maps to `unsupported`.

## Ordinary Findings Remain Separate

Each member case keeps its ordinary effect-claim verdict. ODC runs from sealed offline inputs after ordinary effect analysis and may append diagnostic insight.

ODC cannot alter ordinary effects, evidence, findings, verdicts, order, identity, or an otherwise sufficient exit.

## Interpretation Boundary

ODC reports an association between executed decision outcomes and projected runtime effects. It does not claim causal proof or behavioral correctness.
