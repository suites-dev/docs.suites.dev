## Extend the System Suite

The executable suite still drives the system and owns its native assertions. Blackbox adds evidence about what the running system did across supported boundaries.

| Part                     | Responsibility                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **System or E2E suite**  | Perform actions and check visible outcomes.                                  |
| **Runtime observations** | Record supported OpenTelemetry signals during a finite attempt.              |
| **Effects**              | Normalize eligible observations into portable boundary facts.                |
| **Evidence integrity**   | Establish whether those facts are attributable and usable for a claim.       |
| **Findings**             | State what the Analyzer concluded from accepted claims and bounded evidence. |

A passing assertion and a violated effect claim can coexist. A failed assertion also does not erase effects that occurred before the failure.

## Bound Every Conclusion

Managed capture starts fresh for every case attempt, retry, and sequence member. Sequence members may share declared application state, but they do not share attempts or evidence.

| Evidence                                         | Supported conclusion                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A matching eligible effect exists**            | Establish presence or disprove a forbidden-effect claim.                       |
| **No match exists in complete bounded evidence** | May establish absence or another completeness-dependent result.                |
| **Evidence is incomplete**                       | Cannot establish absence, upper-bound satisfaction, or exactness satisfaction. |

Incomplete evidence produces an inconclusive or integrity finding. It never turns an empty observation into success.

## Keep Repairs Separate From Product Decisions

Blackbox keeps accepted intent, the executable suite, runtime evidence, and findings separate. A coding agent can repair the suite or implementation and rerun the same accepted behavior.

Changing accepted Gherkin, effect policy, evidence requirements, or gate policy returns to a person.

## Keep Observable Decision Coverage Separate

**Observable Decision Coverage** compares accepted decision outcomes with projected effect signatures across exact declared cases.

ODC cannot erase, rewrite, or strengthen an ordinary effect finding.
