Suppose the product-cache case fails and retries. The retry receives a fresh attempt and fresh evidence. Evidence from the first run cannot complete a claim for the second.

**Runtime evidence** is the sealed, identity-bound record Blackbox may analyze for one physical attempt. It includes observed work and the control facts that establish what those observations can prove.

## Bound One Attempt at a Time

<img
  src="/img/blackbox/attempt-evidence-boundary.svg"
  alt="The original attempt and its retry have separate sandbox, capture, execution, and evidence boundaries. No evidence moves from the first attempt into the retry."
  style="display: block; width: 100%; height: auto;"
/>

Every independent attempt owns a fresh sandbox lease, runtime identities, capture, evidence, and teardown. Every retry and ordered-sequence member also receives fresh attempt and evidence identities.

An explicit ordered group may share only its declared application state. Its members remain separately verdictable and keep separate evidence.

## Combine Two Evidence Planes

| Plane                 | Carries                                                                         |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Telemetry**         | Eligible OTLP operations observed during the attempt                            |
| **Assurance control** | Participation, capability, completion, loss, cutoff, settlement, and seal facts |

Neither plane can replace the other. Traces show supported runtime work. Control facts establish whether Blackbox observed enough of the declared scope to evaluate a claim.

## Establish Eligibility Before a Verdict

| Condition        | Question                                                                                     | Result when unmet                                         |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fresh scope**  | Did this physical attempt receive its own lease, window, and capture?                        | Integrity failure                                         |
| **Identity**     | Do the plan, case, realization, attempt, participants, artifacts, and evidence join exactly? | Integrity failure                                         |
| **Capability**   | Can the admitted providers observe the declared claim scope?                                 | Unsupported                                               |
| **Completeness** | Did the required lifecycle close without relevant loss or unresolved work?                   | Inconclusive where the conclusion depends on completeness |

Blackbox preserves conclusive directions. Incomplete evidence can still establish a positive lower bound or a violation already visible in eligible observations. It cannot establish absence, upper-bound satisfaction, or exactness satisfaction from an empty or low count.

## Keep Capture and Analysis Separate

Runtime owns attempts, lifecycle, identities, and evidence. The Analyzer evaluates accepted claims against sealed evidence offline. It does not query or mutate the running system.
