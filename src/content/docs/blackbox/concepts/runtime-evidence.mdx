Suppose the checkout case observes two payment requests and retries after repair. The retry receives a fresh attempt and
fresh evidence. Evidence from the first run cannot complete a claim for the second.

**Runtime evidence** is the observed effects and capture information Blackbox records for one test attempt. Blackbox
fixes that record before analysis so a finding does not depend on changing runtime state.

## Fresh Scope Prevents Evidence Leakage

<img
  src="/img/blackbox/attempt-evidence-boundary.svg"

  style="display: block; width: 100%; height: auto;"
/>

Every independent attempt owns a fresh runtime and evidence boundary. Every retry and ordered-sequence member also
receives fresh attempt and evidence identities.

An explicit ordered group may share only its declared application state. Its members remain separately verdictable and keep separate evidence.

## Observations Need a Capture Boundary

OpenTelemetry traces reveal supported runtime activity. Capture records establish which observations belong to the
attempt and which conclusions the evidence can support.

Neither side can replace the other. A trace can show that a payment request occurred, but it cannot by itself establish
that capture covered the complete claim scope. Capture records can establish a sound boundary, but they cannot invent an
effect that was not observed.

## Incomplete Evidence Limits the Result

Suppose the behavioral contract allows at most one payment request and Blackbox observes two. Those observations already
establish a violation. If capture is incomplete and only one request appears, the evidence cannot establish that no
second request occurred.

This asymmetry prevents missing evidence from becoming a pass. [Look up the exact evidence conditions and claim
outcomes](/docs/blackbox/reference/effects-and-claims#evidence-limits-the-conclusion).

## Keep Capture and Analysis Separate

Runtime owns attempts, lifecycle, identities, and evidence. The Analyzer evaluates accepted claims against sealed evidence offline. It does not query or mutate the running system.
