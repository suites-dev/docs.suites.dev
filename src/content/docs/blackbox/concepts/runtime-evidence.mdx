Suppose a checkout case runs, fails, and retries. Runtime opens fresh capture for the retry. Evidence from the first attempt cannot complete a claim for the second.

**Runtime evidence** is the sealed record of what supported capture observed during one bounded attempt. It preserves the facts Blackbox may analyze and the limits on what those facts can establish.

An **execution** is the act of running a case. An **attempt** is one bounded execution instance with its own capture and evidence. A retry creates a new attempt.

**Managed capture** is the Blackbox-controlled observation scope opened for that attempt.

## Bound One Attempt at a Time

Managed capture starts fresh for every case attempt, retry, and sequence member.

<img
  src="/img/blackbox/attempt-evidence-boundary.svg"
  alt="The original attempt and its retry use separate capture and evidence boundaries. Evidence from the first attempt cannot cross into the retry, and each sequence member also receives its own boundary."
  style="display: block; width: 100%; height: auto;"
/>

Sequence members may share declared application state. They do not share attempts or evidence. Blackbox never merges separate attempts to strengthen a claim.

## Establish Four Evidence Conditions

| Condition              | Checkout question                                                                         | Bounded result when unmet                         |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fresh scope**        | Did new capture open for this retry attempt?                                              | Integrity failure.                                |
| **Identity**           | Do the case, realization, attempt, participating service, and evidence identifiers agree? | Integrity failure.                                |
| **Capture capability** | Could declared capture observe the payment boundary?                                      | Unsupported for claims that need that capability. |
| **Completeness**       | Did payment-boundary capture finish cleanly for this attempt?                             | Inconclusive for completeness-dependent claims.   |

Blackbox does not reinterpret foreign or partial evidence as an empty successful run. Incomplete evidence cannot establish completeness-dependent absence, upper-bound satisfaction, or exactness satisfaction.

## Separate Capture From Analysis

Runtime owns attempts, capture lifecycle, identities, and evidence. It records and seals supported facts. The Analyzer later evaluates accepted claims against that evidence.
