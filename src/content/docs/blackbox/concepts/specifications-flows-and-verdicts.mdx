An accepted checkout scenario says one completed order produces at most one charge request:

```gherkin
Scenario: Do not charge a completed order twice
  Given checkout encounters a transient timeout
  When the order completes after a retry
  Then one order is confirmed
  And no more than one charge request is sent
```

Gherkin makes human intent readable. A person approves the scenario, effect claims, and analysis policy. **SuitePlan** owns the accepted meaning compiled from those approved inputs.

The system suite realizes that meaning, Runtime records what happened, and the Analyzer evaluates the evidence. None of those later stages may silently reinterpret what the person approved.

<Aside type="info" title="Alpha Target">
  The Alpha workflow uses Gherkin as the readable expression of approved human
  intent. Gherkin guides realization; it is not runtime evidence and cannot
  change an effect finding.
</Aside>

## Keep the Chain Directional

<img
  src="/img/blackbox/human-intent-directional-chain.svg"
  alt="A one-way chain moves from human intent to accepted meaning, concrete realization, declared selection, runtime evidence, and findings and verdicts. Later stages cannot redefine earlier authority."
  style="display: block; width: 100%; height: auto;"
/>

Each stage has one owner and a limit:

| Stage                     | Owner        | Owns                                                              | Cannot                                                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Human intent**          | Person       | Approved Gherkin, effect claims, and analysis policy.             | Be inferred or rewritten from observed behavior.                 |
| **Accepted meaning**      | SuitePlan    | The accepted meaning compiled from that approved intent.          | Be redefined by realization or execution.                        |
| **Concrete realization**  | RunnerBundle | The actions and native assertions that execute the accepted case. | Add, remove, or weaken accepted meaning.                         |
| **Declared selection**    | RunPlan      | Selection of an already declared realization.                     | Invent meaning or an undeclared realization.                     |
| **Runtime evidence**      | Runtime      | Attempts, capture lifecycle, identities, and sealed evidence.     | Decide whether observed behavior is acceptable.                  |
| **Findings and verdicts** | Analyzer     | Deterministic analysis of accepted inputs and sealed evidence.    | Change human intent, accepted meaning, realization, or evidence. |

A **finding** is the Analyzer's result for one accepted claim. Its **verdict** is the terminal status. [See the available verdicts](/docs/blackbox/concepts/effects-and-catalogs#read-findings-and-verdicts).

## Preserve Identity Across Representations

Stable identities connect the accepted case, its declared realization, each runtime attempt, and the resulting evidence. Similar titles or nearby source order are not enough.

This prevents evidence from one case, retry, or sequence member from being used to complete another case's claim.

## Repair Without Redefining Intent

The executable system suite and implementation can be repaired when they are missing, stale, or incorrect. Repair does not grant permission to change human intent or accepted meaning.

A proposed change to Gherkin, effect claims, or analysis policy returns to a person.

## Keep Runtime Facts Directional

Runtime opens a fresh managed-capture boundary and records supported observations from the running system. The effects catalog can normalize eligible observations into portable effects.

Those effects become part of sealed evidence before the Analyzer compares the accepted claims and analysis policy with that evidence. Runtime facts never become human intent or accepted meaning automatically.
